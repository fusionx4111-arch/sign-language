INDIAN SIGN LANGUAGE (ISL) ALPHABET - GESTURE STEPS
Source: ISLRTC chart (descriptions written from the line drawings; verify fine finger details against the official chart)

GENERAL SETUP
Hold your hands in front of your chest, in view of the camera. Make each shape, then hold it still for a second.

A
1. Make two fists.
2. Extend both index fingers.
3. Touch the fingertips together to form a roof peak.

B
1. Raise both hands in front of you.
2. Touch the thumb and index fingertips of each hand.
3. Join the two hands so they form a diamond, other fingers curled.

C
1. Raise one hand.
2. Curve the fingers and thumb.
3. Leave the gap facing sideways to form a "C".

D
1. Raise one index finger straight up.
2. Curve the other hand's thumb and fingers into a rounded shape.
3. Hold it beside the raised finger.

E
1. Extend one index finger.
2. Extend the other index finger.
3. Rest one against the other at an angle.

F
1. Extend both index fingers.
2. Lay one across the other.
3. Hold the cross steady.

G
1. Make a fist with each hand.
2. Stack one fist on top of the other.
3. Hold them close together.

H
1. Hold one palm flat.
2. Hold the other palm flat.
3. Lay one hand across the back of the other.

I
1. Point one index finger.
2. Bring it up to your cheek.
3. Touch the cheek lightly and hold.

J
1. Shape one hand into a hook with thumb and index finger.
2. Extend the other index finger.
3. Touch or trace it along the hook.

K
1. Point one index finger up.
2. Stick the other hand's thumb out.
3. Press the hands together to form the "K" angle.

L
1. Raise one hand.
2. Point the index finger up.
3. Extend the thumb sideways to form an "L".

M
1. Hold one palm open and flat.
2. Take three fingers (index, middle, ring) of the other hand.
3. Lay them across the palm.

N
1. Hold one palm open and flat.
2. Take two fingers (index, middle) of the other hand.
3. Lay them across the palm.

O
1. Raise one hand.
2. Curve all fingers toward the thumb.
3. Touch the fingertips to the thumb to make a round "O".

P
1. Point one index finger up.
2. Touch thumb and index fingertip of the other hand to make a circle.
3. Place the circle at the top of the raised finger.

Q
1. Point one index finger up.
2. Curl the other hand's thumb and finger into a small hook.
3. Place the hook at the base of the raised finger.

R
1. Hold one palm open.
2. Touch thumb and index fingertip of the other hand together.
3. Rest them on the open palm.

S
1. Make a fist.
2. Form a small loop with thumb and index finger.
3. Support it with the other hand from the side.

T
1. Point one index finger straight up.
2. Extend the other index finger sideways.
3. Lay it flat across the top to make a "T".

U
1. Raise one hand.
2. Lift the index and middle fingers, held together.
3. Curl the other fingers down.

V
1. Raise one hand.
2. Lift the index and middle fingers.
3. Spread them apart in a "V" and curl the rest.

W
1. Open the fingers of both hands.
2. Bring the hands together.
3. Cross the fingers over each other to form a "W".

X
1. Extend both index fingers.
2. Cross them at the middle.
3. Hold to form an "X".

Y
1. Extend both index fingers.
2. Angle them toward each other.
3. Cross them diagonally so they form a "Y".

Z
1. Hold one index finger straight up.
2. Slope the other hand flat.
3. Lay it diagonally across the finger like a "Z" stroke.
