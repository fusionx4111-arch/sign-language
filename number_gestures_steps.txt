NUMBER GESTURES 1 TO 9 - STEP-BY-STEP GUIDE
============================================

General rule: keep the palm facing the viewer and the wrist relaxed.
For 6 to 9 you use TWO hands: the second hand always shows 5 (fully open),
and the first hand shows 1, 2, 3 or 4. Total = 5 + first hand's count.


GESTURE 1
---------
Step 1: Make a loose fist with one hand.
Step 2: Raise only the index finger straight up.
Step 3: Keep the middle, ring and little fingers folded into the palm.
Step 4: Keep the thumb folded over the folded fingers.


GESTURE 2
---------
Step 1: Make a loose fist with one hand.
Step 2: Raise the index finger and the middle finger.
Step 3: Spread these two fingers apart to form a V shape.
Step 4: Keep the ring finger, little finger and thumb folded.


GESTURE 3
---------
Step 1: Start with a closed fist.
Step 2: Raise the index finger and the middle finger and spread them in a V.
Step 3: Extend the thumb out to the side.
Step 4: Keep the ring finger and little finger folded into the palm.


GESTURE 4
---------
Step 1: Open the hand.
Step 2: Raise the index, middle, ring and little fingers straight up and slightly apart.
Step 3: Fold the thumb across the palm.


GESTURE 5
---------
Step 1: Open the hand fully.
Step 2: Spread all four fingers apart.
Step 3: Extend the thumb out to the side.


GESTURE 6 (two hands)
---------------------
First hand:
Step 1: Make a fist.
Step 2: Stick the thumb out to the side (counts as 1).
Second hand:
Step 3: Open the hand fully with all five fingers spread (counts as 5).
Step 4: Hold both hands side by side, palms facing the viewer.


GESTURE 7 (two hands)
---------------------
First hand:
Step 1: Make a fist.
Step 2: Raise the index finger straight up.
Step 3: Stick the thumb out to the side (counts as 2).
Second hand:
Step 4: Open the hand fully with all five fingers spread (counts as 5).
Step 5: Hold both hands side by side, palms facing the viewer.


GESTURE 8 (two hands)
---------------------
First hand:
Step 1: Make a fist.
Step 2: Raise the index finger and the middle finger.
Step 3: Stick the thumb out to the side (counts as 3).
Step 4: Keep the ring finger and little finger folded.
Second hand:
Step 5: Open the hand fully with all five fingers spread (counts as 5).
Step 6: Hold both hands side by side, palms facing the viewer.


GESTURE 9 (two hands)
---------------------
First hand:
Step 1: Raise the index, middle, ring and little fingers straight up.
Step 2: Fold the thumb across the palm (counts as 4).
Second hand:
Step 3: Open the hand fully with all five fingers spread (counts as 5).
Step 4: Hold both hands side by side, palms facing the viewer.


QUICK SUMMARY
-------------
1 = index finger
2 = index + middle (V)
3 = index + middle + thumb out
4 = four fingers up, thumb folded
5 = open hand
6 = thumb-out fist + open hand
7 = index up + thumb out + open hand
8 = index + middle up + thumb out + open hand
9 = four fingers up + open hand
